Frequently Asked Questions

What does Nomhle do?

Nomhle is an Independent Forever Business Owner who helps customers explore Forever products and supports people who are interested in learning more about the Forever business opportunity.

Where are you based?

I'm based in East London, South Africa, but my Forever business is not limited to one local community. Contact me and I'll guide you according to your location.

How do I order products?

Browse the products you're interested in and contact me directly. Once my official Forever shopping link is connected to the website, you'll also be able to continue through the appropriate Forever shopping process.

Can you help me choose products?

Yes. Tell me what type of products you're looking for and I'll help you navigate the available range.

Can I also become a Forever Business Owner?

If you'd like to learn more about the business opportunity, contact me and I'll explain how it works and what the next steps involve.

Is income guaranteed?

No. Individual results vary. Building any business requires effort, commitment, skills and consistent activity.
